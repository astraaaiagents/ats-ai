Light Theme Benefits
Better in bright office environments. Lower perceived cognitive load. Familiar B2B SaaS aesthetic. Easier to read dense data tables. More accessible (higher contrast ratios).
Recommendation: B. Warm Indigo
The warm off-white base (#faf9f7) reduces eye strain vs. pure white. Indigo primary (#4f46e5) is distinctive without being flashy. Warm gray borders feel softer than cool slate. Premium Stripe/Notion vibe.

Which palette works? I recommend B. Warm Indigo — it's professional, modern, and easier on the eyes for all-day use. Once confirmed, I'll write the full design doc.
