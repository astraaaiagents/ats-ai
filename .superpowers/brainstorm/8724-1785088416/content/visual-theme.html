Color Palette Options

Dark mode is default (recruiters work long hours). Each palette has 5 semantic colors + neutral scale.

A. Corporate Navy Conservative, bank-grade, trustworthy
Vibe: Bloomberg Terminal meets Linear. Authoritative, calm, high info-density.
B. Indigo Modern Recommended — tech-forward, warm-professional
Vibe: Linear meets Superhuman. Distinctive, professional, memorable. Indigo signals intelligence.
C. Slate Professional Minimal, monochrome, content-forward
Vibe: Notion / Linear minimal. Content is king. Low visual noise, maximum data density. Recommended for power users who spend 8+ hours in the tool.

AI Output Styling (EU AI Act Requirement)

AI-GENERATED Confidence: 92%
All AI-generated content is clearly labeled with a left border accent, badge, and confidence score.
LOW CONFIDENCE Confidence: 54%
Low-confidence assertions (<70%) use a yellow badge and require manual recruiter confirmation before actions.
AGENT SUGGESTION Proactive
Proactive agent suggestions (not directly asked for) use a distinct purple/tint to signal unsolicited vs. requested output.
