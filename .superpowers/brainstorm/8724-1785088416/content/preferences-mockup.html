Design Principles
Full transparency — every learned preference is visible with confidence level and evidence count.
One-click override — remove or adjust any learned preference.
Weekly digest — auto-generated summary of preference drift vs. last week.
Natural language — set preferences by talking to the agent ("Remember, I prefer...").
Confidence Levels
🟢 High → 20+ consistent actions, auto-applied
🟡 Medium → 10-19 actions, suggests but flags
⚪ Low → <10 actions, shows but not applied

Low-confidence patterns are visible but don't affect ranking until confirmed.
